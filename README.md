# ZAlpha-AI-Student-Navigator
https://ai.studio/apps/4b5bcce4-41e6-44ca-8929-10ea18041952
🚀 **Introducing ZAlpha — AI Opportunity Navigator | AZINHACK ’26**

Students often miss valuable opportunities, not because they lack potential, but because they lack the right information and guidance.

That’s the problem we aim to solve with **ZAlpha** 💡

🎯 **Our project focuses on 3 major challenges:**

🔎 **1. Opportunity Discovery**
Students may not know all the entrance exams, alternative pathways, scholarships, and admission opportunities available for their goals.

📄 **2. Document & Eligibility Clarity**
Confusion about required documents, validity, eligibility criteria, and application rules can lead to missed opportunities.

💻 **3. Engineering Career Roadmaps**
Undergraduate students often struggle to decide which skills to learn, which projects to build, and how to prepare for internships and competitive placements.

✨ **Our proposed solution: ZAlpha**

An AI-powered student guidance platform designed to help students discover opportunities, understand official requirements, and build personalized career roadmaps.

🛠️ **Technology planned for the prototype:**

* **Replit AI:** Application development and prototyping
* **TinyFish Search API:** Live web search for relevant information
* **TinyFish Fetch API:** Retrieving webpage content for deeper
